Getting There

By air

Two airports serve the Bay Area. Oakland (OAK) is the closest to Berkeley — about 20 minutes by car or BART. San Francisco (SFO) is a great option too, roughly 45 minutes away. Both have plenty of direct flights from across the US and beyond.

Lodging

We recommend staying in the Berkeley area to be close to the main venue. There are lovely hotels, as well as plenty of Airbnb and Vrbo options that work beautifully for solo travelers and groups alike.
